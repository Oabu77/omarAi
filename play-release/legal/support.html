Support contact pending confirmation. Do not publish this as the Play support page until [[CONFIRM_SUPPORT_EMAIL]] is replaced by a monitored DarCloud address and the staged notice/robots block are removed.

What this release supports

  • Local customer, lead, job, and invoice records
  • Dashboard summaries calculated from local records
  • Local request routing and truthful disconnected task status
  • Android speech recognition, camera, Photo Picker, and file picker controls
  • Local JSON export and local database deletion

What is not connected

This build does not provide live AI answers, an Omar AI account/sign-in, paid entitlement, phone calls, linked financial accounts, user messaging, marketplace booking, customer payment, file/photo analysis, or company/legal filing.

Common issues

The app says the Omar AI service is disconnected

That is the expected state of v0.1.0. The request is saved locally as Planned; no remote analysis or action occurred.

Voice input does not start

Confirm that your device has an enabled speech-recognition service and that the service's own Android permission settings allow voice input. Omar AI itself does not request microphone permission. Typed input remains available.

Camera input does not start

Confirm camera permission in Android Settings. You can instead choose a photo or continue with text.

Pro or Business cannot be purchased

Paid purchase is intentionally unavailable without a production backend verifier. No local workaround or manual payment is offered.

I want to remove my data

Follow Delete local data. There is no Omar AI account in this release.
