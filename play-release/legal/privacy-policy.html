Omar AI Privacy Policy

1. Current release scope

Omar AI v0.1.0 is a local-first Android workspace. It has no Omar AI account, sign-in, connected AI-answer service, remote file analysis, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting service, push notifications, customer payments, or live phone, messaging, finance, marketplace, or filing integration. The Omar AI backend URL is not configured in this build.

3. Device and third-party services

  • Android speech recognition: When you tap the microphone, the speech-recognition service selected on your device captures speech and returns text to Omar AI. Omar AI does not receive or save raw audio. The speech provider may process data under its own privacy terms.
  • Google Play: Google handles app installation. This build has no in-app subscription-management link and does not include the Play Billing SDK, query products or purchase history, start or restore an Omar AI purchase, receive a purchase token, or activate Pro/Business. It does not collect raw card data. Google Play handles its own service under Google's terms.
  • External browser/Play app: A link opens only after you tap it and is then handled by Android, your browser, or Google Play.

DarCloud does not sell personal information, use it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or include an advertising SDK in this release.

4. Android permissions

  • Camera: requested only after you choose camera input.
  • Voice input: launches the speech-recognition service installed on your device. Omar AI does not request microphone permission itself; the selected speech service controls its own permission and privacy handling.
  • Internet: reserved for a future configured Omar AI service; that service remains disconnected and Play Billing is not bundled in this artifact. Signed-build network testing must confirm that current app flows initiate no unexpected traffic.
  • Photos and documents: selected through Android system pickers without broad photo-library or storage permission.

The app does not request location, contacts, SMS, call logs, phone state, notification, broad media/storage, Accessibility Service, or Advertising ID permission.

5. Retention and deletion

Local database records remain until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Android backup is disabled for Omar AI app data. The in-app Delete local data control removes supported local database records—customers, leads, jobs, invoices, conversations, tasks, audit events, and output reports—and attempts to remove Omar AI camera-preview cache files and any legacy persisted picker grants. Use Android Clear storage or uninstall if you need to remove all app-private data.

The app also attempts to remove a camera preview when you remove it from a draft, process the draft, or leave the Home screen. An original photo or document selected from outside Omar AI is not deleted by Omar AI. See Delete local data for exact steps.

6. Security

Local records use Android app-private storage, Android backup is disabled, and release configuration prohibits cleartext network traffic. This release does not claim end-to-end encryption, encrypted local database fields, an independent security certification, or a live DarCloud cloud service. No method is perfectly secure; protect your unlocked device and avoid entering information unnecessary for your work.

7. Your choices

You can skip camera, voice, photo, and file controls; revoke Omar AI camera permission or adjust your device speech service's access in Android settings; export supported records; delete supported local records in the app; clear all app storage in Android settings; or uninstall the app. There is no Omar AI account or remote account data to delete in this version.

8. Children

Omar AI v0.1.0 is intended for adults age 18 and older and is not directed to children.

9. Changes

We will revise this policy before enabling accounts, connected AI processing, uploads, subscriptions, analytics, advertising, or other remote data use. The revised date will be posted and additional notice or consent provided when required.
